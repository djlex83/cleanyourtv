# glashaus wallpaper

`wallpaper.jpg` is the night standby screen of [glashaus-vis2](https://github.com/djlex83/glashaus-vis2),
an ioBroker VIS 2 dashboard by the theme author, djlex83: the dotted house drawing with its solar roof,
trees, a cloud and the sun over a faint "zuhause", rendered from that project at 1920x1080 on its dark
background (#111110). The clock and the value tiles are left out, as the launcher draws its own clock.
glashaus-vis2 is released under the MIT licence, so the repository can redistribute the picture.
